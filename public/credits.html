Instrument sounds

FluidR3 General MIDI — Frank Wen. MP3 rendering: Benjamin Gleitzman / MIDI.js Soundfonts.

MIDI.js Soundfonts source · Creative Commons Attribution 3.0

We extract C1–C7 samples for 34 instrument sounds and adjust pitch, volume and duration during playback. Wind and bowed sounds use crossfades to sustain notes, and each instrument can be positioned in space. These are not recordings of a live orchestra performance. Their inclusion does not imply the creators’ endorsement or official support.
